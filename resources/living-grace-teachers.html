Field Story

Meet the Teachers at Living Grace

October 2026

Living Grace Enhance Skills School is a small community school in Thailand. It helps disadvantaged children keep learning while building basic skills and confidence.

A small school with a clear purpose

The school's aim is simple: help children from difficult circumstances continue to attend class, and build foundational skills and self-confidence alongside their studies.

Why we support it

Like our other partners, Living Grace is led locally. We support the teachers' work and let them decide what the children need most.

Teacher profiles
