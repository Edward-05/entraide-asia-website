Living Grace Enhance Skills School

A small community school in Thailand that helps disadvantaged children keep learning while building foundational skills and confidence.

Our Partner

A small school with a clear purpose

Living Grace is led locally. Its teachers welcome children into warm, simple classrooms where lessons mix language practice, music, games and time together.

Like our other partners, we support the teachers' work and let them decide what the children need most.
